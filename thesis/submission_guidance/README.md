## Before initial examination submission

- [ ] Confirm the registered award, candidature deadline and intended submission date with the School. The thesis currently says November 2026.
- [ ] Confirm that Notice of Presentation was submitted at least two months before the intended submission date (2026/27 regulations, 19.1). If this has not happened, contact the School now about the timetable.
- [ ] Obtain the current Hub guide and forms; check format, word limit, required declarations and delivery instructions against that guide.
- [ ] Arrange the written own-work confirmation and supervisor countersignature (19.7). The declaration printed inside the thesis does not replace the separate form.
- [ ] Confirm ethics approval 12425, prior-publication/submission statements, any collaborative contributions and the required AI-use acknowledgement with the author and supervisors.
- [ ] Generate and review the final thesis Turnitin report. Section 21.2 requires student submission to Turnitin, except that the PGR Director or nominee performs the check when there are two external examiners. The report must accompany the thesis.
- [ ] Review individual matches for attributed quotations, bibliography, prior work, appendix example outputs and unattributed wording. A percentage alone does not establish plagiarism or its absence. No universal acceptable percentage has been established by this audit.
- [ ] Check final PDF contents, lists, tables, figures, references and pagination. Keep the editable DOCX available if requested.
- [ ] Confirm the submission channel with SEE PGR support and retain the submission receipt. Older public instructions and the Library FAQ identify SA-PGR@salford.ac.uk, copying School PGR support and the supervisor; verify that this remains the current route in the Hub guide.

## How to obtain the Turnitin report

Sign in to Salford Blackboard and look for the SPoRT module's Draft Submission Area. If it is unavailable, ask SEE PGR support for access and the correct thesis Turnitin link. Confirm whether that draft area supplies the report accepted for final submission or whether a separate final check is required. Upload the agreed final thesis version, open the similarity report, inspect its matches and download the report using the options available in the University interface. Keep the report and receipt tied to that exact thesis version.

There is no public pre-existing originality report for this thesis. This audit has no authenticated Salford access and has not submitted the thesis or generated a Turnitin report.

## After examination and corrections

Follow the examiners' correction requirements and obtain the required approval. Obtain the current Declaration 2 and deposit instructions from the Hub. Deposit the approved thesis through Worktribe into USIR, resolve copyright/licensing and any approved access restrictions, and retain the deposit receipt for the award process. These are later steps, distinct from initial examination submission.
